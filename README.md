# New Product Development (NPD)

## Definition
New Product Development (NPD) is the process of bringing a new product to the market, from idea generation to commercialization.

## Stages of NPD
1. Idea Generation
2. Idea Screening
3. Concept Development and Testing
4. Business Analysis
5. Product Development
6. Market Testing
7. Commercialization

## Description
This repository explains the structured approach used by enterprises to develop new products efficiently while minimizing risk and maximizing market success.
